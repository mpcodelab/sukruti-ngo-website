About Us

Sukruti Swayam Seva Samsthe is a grassroots NGO working in rural and urban development. We focus on empowering communities through health, education, livelihood, skill development, and sustainable initiatives.

02

Vision

To create empowered, self-sustaining communities through inclusive growth and holistic development in rural and urban India.

03

Mission

To catalyze social change by implementing effective programs in health, education, sanitation, skill development, and livelihood, especially for marginalized communities.

ORGANIZATION PROFILE

Reaching Services to the Unreached

Sukruti Swayam Seva Samsthe (R) is a non-profit organization, which is working in various areas of society like Health, Education and Livelihood, Water and Sanitation, Financial Literacy, Dairy Development, Skill and Rural development.

Sukruti working with a vision to “Reach the services to the unreached area”. Organization strong presence in Haveri, Koppal, Belagavi, Dharwad, Bagalakot and Raichur.
