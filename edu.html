Our Education Programs

Sukruti works with rural communities to promote children's education and create awareness about the harmful effects of child labor.

CHILD EDUCATION & AWARENESS

Child Labor and Education Awareness

The organization conducted 2 programs on Child Labor and Education Awareness in remote villages of Koppal taluk.

Officials from the Education Department were invited to sensitize communities about the importance of protecting children and ensuring their access to education.

Key Areas of Awareness

  • 01
    Negative impacts of child labor

    Creating awareness about the negative impacts of child labor on children.

  • 02
    Legal consequences

    Helping communities understand the legal consequences of sending children for work.

  • 03
    Importance of education

    Encouraging families and communities to understand the importance of children's education.

EDUCATION SUPPORT

Supporting Children With Learning Materials

Programs were held in Alwandi, with 56 participants, and Kardigudda, with 38 participants.

Sukruti also distributed shoes and school materials to children with support from TOMS and IDFC Foundation.

These efforts aim to encourage children to continue their education and support families in creating a positive learning environment.
